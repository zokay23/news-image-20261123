- **Ukraine sinks a Russian warship in Libya** — sea drones sink the corvette Merkury at Benghazi, 2,400 km from the front; a cargo ship and a helicopter were hit too.
- **15 killed as Russia strikes the Kyiv region** — a drone flattens a five-storey block in Dymer; 11 dead there, a boy of seven pulled alive after 22 hours.
- **COP31 ends without a deal** — the longest climate summit on record closes in deadlock over fossil fuels.
- **Huang is back — Nvidia shares leap 33%** as the board reinstates Jensen Huang after Trump pressure; the Nasdaq rallies 6%.
- **Factory fire kills at least 24** in Banten, Indonesia.
- **Bangladesh vows to jail Hasina if she returns**; **India pledges $1.6bn to flood-hit Nepal**; **Guterres urges global rules for AI**.
- **Sport:** Cowboys rout Titans 51–30, 49ers crush Vikings 35–10, and the Mets sign Skubal for $450M.

## How it works

- `build_poster.py` draws `entry.png` (1024×1024, deterministic — run it twice and the bytes are identical) with Python + Pillow, no network.
- `main.py` copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` byte-for-byte.
